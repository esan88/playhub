The Food we Eat

Food, riddles & sharing

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Poori with aloo sabzi, roti, idli with chutney, paratha, chilla — so many lunch boxes! We solve food riddles, clap syllables (far–mer, two claps) and learn that food tastes better when we share it and say thank you.

  • Riddle answers: orange, coconut, onion, grapes — can you solve them?
  • Clap once for one syllable: man, cat, hut. Clap twice: far-mer, car-rot.
  • New words: have, thank you, share, lunch, sick, fruits, today, smiling.
  • When a friend is hungry, we SHARE our food — and say THANK YOU.
