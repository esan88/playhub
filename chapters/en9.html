Anandi’s Rainbow

Colours of the rainbow

📖 What you will learn

After the rain, a huge bright rainbow crosses the blue sky! Anandi paints her garden flowers with its colours and leaves yellow for the shining Sun. Put the seven bands in order — red on top, violet at the bottom.

  • Seven colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet.
  • Red is the top band of the rainbow — violet is the last.
  • Anandi’s cat is called Milli; the Sun kept its bright yellow.
  • After the rain comes the rainbow — colours hide when it is gone.
