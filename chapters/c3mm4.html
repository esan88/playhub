📖 What you will learn

Holidays! We are going to Nani Maa’s village by train. Tickets, bags, station, train — a journey has steps, and every step comes in order! 🚂

Big tasks become easy when we order their steps: first, next, then and last.

  • A journey is a list of steps in order.
  • First we plan, then we pack, then we travel.
  • Tickets tell the date, time and seat number.
  • Count days on a calendar to know trip length.
