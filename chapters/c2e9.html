Oh no! A little fly forgot his own name! He asks the ant, the tree, the cow, the grass and the leaf — and when the wind lifts the fallen leaf, he remembers at last: My name is Fly!

  • Your name is your identity.
  • Ants climb trees and cows rest in the shade.
  • Cows eat grass and leaves fall on grass.
  • Wind can lift a light leaf into the air.
