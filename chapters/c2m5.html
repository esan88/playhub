Playing with Lines

Standing, sleeping, slanting lines

📖 What you will learn

Yoga friends stand straight, sleep flat and bend in curves! Find standing, sleeping, slanting and curved lines with thread and dots — then paint Warli art with them.

  • Lines can stand, sleep, slant or curve.
  • Standing lines go up-down; sleeping lines go side-to-side.
  • Curved lines make clouds and rainbows.
  • Warli and Madhubani art use many types of lines.
