📖 What you will learn

It’s Raksha Bandhan! Sisters tie colourful rakhis in red-gold, red-gold patterns while brothers count sweets for everyone. 🎀

Patterns repeat a rule. Count in groups to find totals fast.

  • A pattern repeats its rule again and again.
  • Red, gold, red, gold is an ABAB pattern.
  • Count rakhis in groups of 2s, 5s or 10s.
  • One more repeat extends any pattern.
