Two friends and a bear

📖 What you will learn

Two friends cross a forest when a bear appears. One climbs the tree, one lies still. The bear teaches who a true friend is.

This tale shows a friend in need is a friend indeed. The boy on the ground holds his breath like the dead. The bear sniffs and goes away.

  • Two friends walk through a forest.
  • One friend climbs a tree in fear.
  • The other lies still holding breath.
  • A true friend helps in trouble.
