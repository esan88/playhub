पेड़ों की अम्मा ‘थिमक्का’

Thimmakka ne lagaye sainkadon pedh

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Thimmakka ne sadak kinare peepal lagaye — paani diya, raksha ki. Aaj sainkadon pedh unki santaan hain! 🌳🙏

Thimmakka ne apne jeevan mein sainkadon pedh lagaye. pedh lagana sabse bada daan hai.

  • Thimmakka Karnataka ki paryavaran mitra hain.
  • Unhone sadak kinare pedh lagaye.
  • Roz paani dekar pedhon ko paala.
  • Hum bhi ek pedh lagayen.
