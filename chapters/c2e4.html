Seeing without Seeing

Story: senses help us see

📖 What you will learn

A new boy with black glasses joins Onshangla’s class. Her mother Ava ties a scarf over her eyes — and suddenly touch, smell, hearing and taste show her a whole new world. A gentle story about kindness.

  • Blind people use touch, smell, hearing and taste to learn.
  • Eyes should be kept safe and clean.
  • Be kind and be friends with differently-abled children.
  • Ava is the Naga word for mother.
