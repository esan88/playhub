📖 What you will learn

Door rehne wale dost ko patra likho — haal-chaal, masti, pyaar! Patra dilon ko jodte hain. ✉️❤️

Patra mein dinank, sambodhan, haal-chaal, baat aur naam likhte hain.

  • Sabse upar dinank likho.
  • Pyare dost ko sambodhan likho.
  • Apna haal-chaal batao.
  • Ant mein apna naam likho.
