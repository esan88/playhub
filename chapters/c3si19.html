لومڑی اور کوا

Fox flatters the crow

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

A fox is hungry for two days and sees a crow with bread. She praises his beauty and sweet voice. The crow opens its beak and loses the bread.

This picture story warns against false praise. The fox names rivers Ravi and Jhelum to flatter. Clever words can steal careless gifts.

  • A fox is hungry for two days.
  • A crow holds a whole bread in beak.
  • The fox praises beauty and sweet song.
  • The bread falls as the beak opens.
