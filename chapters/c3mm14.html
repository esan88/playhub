The Surajkund Fair

Money and counting at the fair

📖 What you will learn

Dhol beats, colourful stalls, and the giant wheel! At the Surajkund Fair every ticket and toy has a price — count your rupees right! 🎠

Add prices to find totals. Subtract to find change. Read counts to find what sold most.

  • ₹20 + ₹15 = ₹35 for two tickets.
  • Change = money given minus price.
  • Tally marks count visitors fast.
  • The tallest bar sold the most.
