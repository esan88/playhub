Laughing princess tale

📖 What you will learn

Phool Kumari laughs loudly in the garden, then turns sad and quiet. Bear men and monkey men try to cheer her. A falling cap brings her laugh back.

This tale is about a king, a queen and their dear daughter. When she laughs, flowers, birds and children smile too. Joy spreads to every one around.

  • Phool Kumari is the dear royal daughter.
  • She laughs loudly and then turns sad.
  • Bear and monkey shows try to cheer her.
  • A falling cap and beard make her laugh.
