Hina of Karnal loves the sky and dreams big. Her teacher tells the tale of Kalpana Chawla. Small eyes can dream beyond the stars.

This lesson tells how Kalpana flew on Columbia in 1997 and became the first Indian woman in space. Children remember her hard work and courage. Her memory guides young dreamers.
