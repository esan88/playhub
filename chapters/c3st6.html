Folding a paper boat in order

📖 What you will learn

Rain puddles are oceans for paper boats! Fold one carefully, sail it giggling, and share the joy with friends. ⛵🌧️

Folding needs steps in order. Fold, crease, fold again — then sail!

  • Start with a rectangle of paper.
  • Fold in half and press the crease.
  • Open the bottom to make the boat.
  • Float it gently — paper tears if rushed!
