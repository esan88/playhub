What fun! In this rhyming poem a child pretends to be every animal — leaping like a lamb, climbing like a cat, hopping, swimming, trotting and flying. Act it out as you learn!

  • Lambs leap and cats climb.
  • Frogs hop and fish swim.
  • Horses trot and birds fly.
  • Pretending to be animals is fun exercise.
