Food We Eat

Foods and where they come from

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Breakfast idli, lunch dal-roti, dinner khichdi! Every bite travelled from farm to market to kitchen. Yum — thank the farmer! 🍛

We eat cereals, pulses, vegetables, fruits and milk. Farmers grow them.

  • Roti comes from wheat; rice from paddy.
  • Dal gives us protein to grow strong.
  • Vegetables and fruits give vitamins.
  • Milk comes from cows and buffaloes.
