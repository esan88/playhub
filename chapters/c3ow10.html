📖 What you will learn

Look around your room — pot, chair, book, spoon! Every single thing is made of SOME material. Let’s become material detectives! 🔍

Things are made of materials: clay, wood, metal, glass, paper, cloth, plastic.

  • Pots are made of clay; chairs of wood.
  • Glasses can be steel or glass.
  • Books are paper; shirts are cloth.
  • Same thing can use many materials.
