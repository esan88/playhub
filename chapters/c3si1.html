Look at red flowers and green leaves. This sweet hamd says Allah made them all. Let us sing and thank Allah.

This hamd by Hafeez Jalandhari praises Allah as Khaliq and Malik. Every thing shows His wisdom and blessing. Children read it aloud with love.

  • A hamd praises Allah for all His gifts.
  • Red flowers and green leaves show His art.
  • Allah is Khaliq who makes and Malik who owns.
  • Read the poem aloud in a sweet voice.
