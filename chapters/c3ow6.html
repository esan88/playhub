Living in Harmony

Caring for people, animals, nature

📖 What you will learn

The class feeds birds, waters saplings and helps Grandma cross the road. When everyone cares, the whole world smiles! 🕊️

Harmony means living kindly with people, animals and nature.

  • Feed birds and give animals water.
  • Water plants and do not pluck flowers.
  • Help elders, friends and neighbours.
  • Keep voices soft near sleeping babies and animals.
