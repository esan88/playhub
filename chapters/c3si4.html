بھارت پیارا

Our lovely Bharat song

📖 What you will learn

Bharat is the star of our eyes and support of our hearts. Its flowers, fruits and forests are lovely. Let us sing this song together.

This nazm calls Bharat dear and unique. It praises its water and land, today and tomorrow. Children sing it and learn new words.

  • Bharat is the star of our eyes.
  • Its flowers, fruits and forests are lovely.
  • We sing its praise all together.
  • Love your land, water and people.
