Peels here, paper there! Kabir sorts waste like a superhero — wet in green, dry in blue, and the park stays clean. ♻️🦸

Waste must be separated: wet waste rots into compost; dry waste can be recycled.

  • Fruit peels go in the green wet bin.
  • Paper and plastic go in the blue dry bin.
  • Wet waste becomes compost for plants.
  • Say no to littering — use dustbins!
