ہمدردی

Bulbul and jugnu kindness

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

A lonely bulbul sits sad on a branch as night falls. Tiny jugnu comes with a lamp of light. Helping others is the best work.

This poem by Allama Iqbal teaches kindness. The night is dark and the bulbul fears the way. Jugnu offers his God given light with love.

  • A bulbul sits sad and alone at night.
  • Darkness covers every thing around.
  • Jugnu brings light like a small lamp.
  • Best people help others in need.
