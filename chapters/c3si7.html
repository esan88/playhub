📖 What you will learn

Dark clouds gather and rain falls drip drop. Dry earth wakes up green again. Farmers smile as crops dance.

This nazm of Barsaat shows clouds, rain and joy. Flowers get new colours and jungles turn green. Children learn rain words and rhymes.

  • Black clouds rise before the rain.
  • Rain wakes dry earth to life.
  • Farmers work hard and crops grow.
  • Flowers and jungles turn fresh and green.
