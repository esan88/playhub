A Farm

Farm animals & sounds

📖 What you will learn

Grandpa had a farm — E-I-E-I-O! The cow says moo, the hen says cluck. Meet the animals and birds near Jaggu’s house in Nagpur: a dog at the gate, cats in the verandah, a green parrot and a many-coloured peacock.

  • The cow says MOO, the hen says CLUCK — every animal has a sound.
  • Farm animals: cat, cow, goat, pig, hen, cock, sparrow.
  • Jaggu’s pets: the dog lives near the gate, the cat chases the rat.
  • A parrot is green with a red beak — a peacock has many colours!
