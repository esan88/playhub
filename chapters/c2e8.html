A Show of Clouds

📖 What you will learn

Lie on your back and look up! A child sees a big white bear, a sailing ship and an elephant waving its trunk in the clouds. What shapes can YOU find in the sky?

  • Clouds can look like animals and ships.
  • Clouds can be white, grey or black.
  • The day sky shows sun and clouds; the night sky shows moon and stars.
  • Watching and imagining builds observation.
