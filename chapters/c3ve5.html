Aam ke pedh ki kahani

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Chhoti guthli, dharti mein soyi — paanipeekar jaagi, pedh bani, phir ras bhare aam! pedh lagao, chhaon pao! 🥭

Aam garmi ka raja phal hai. pedh humein phal, chhaon aur lakdi deta hai.

  • Guthli se aam ka pedh ugta hai.
  • pedh ko paani aur dhoop chahiye.
  • Aam garmi mein pakte hain.
  • pedh lagana punya ka kaam hai.
