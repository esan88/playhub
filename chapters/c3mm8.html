Fair Share

Equal sharing and division

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Twelve shiny laddoos, four hungry friends. Deal them round and round — sharing fairly means everyone gets the SAME number! 🍬

Division is fair sharing. Split a total into equal groups.

  • Fair share = every group gets the same number.
  • 12 shared by 4 gives 3 each.
  • Deal one by one to share fairly.
  • Leftovers smaller than the groups stay as remainder.
