The Crow

Story: be yourself

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

A black crow wants to look beautiful, so he sticks colourful peacock feathers on himself! The other crows laugh lovingly and tell him: you are beautiful in black — we love you as you are.

  • Crows are black and peacocks are colourful.
  • Feathers grow on wings, tails and heads.
  • Real beauty is being yourself.
  • Love friends for what they are.
