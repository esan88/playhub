Fun with Shapes

2D and 3D shapes

📖 What you will learn

The shape parade is here! Flat shapes dance on paper while solid shapes roll and stack. Can you name every marcher? 🎪

Flat shapes have sides and corners. Solid shapes have faces, edges and corners — and some can roll!

  • Triangle has 3 sides, square has 4 equal sides.
  • Circle has no sides and no corners.
  • Cube and box stack well; ball and cone roll.
  • Match each solid to its flat face — a cone makes a circle!
