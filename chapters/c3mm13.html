📖 What you will learn

January leads the parade, December waves goodbye! Twelve months march in order — can you line them all up? 📅

A year has 12 months in a fixed order. Calendars and birthdays follow them.

  • January is the first month, December the last.
  • 12 months make 1 full year.
  • 7 days make 1 week — they repeat forever.
  • Festivals and birthdays come in their month’s turn.
