Little Drops of Water

Poem: small acts, big world

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Little drops of water make the mighty ocean, and little grains of sand make pleasant land! This song-poem shows how small words of love and acts of kindness spread smiles everywhere.

  • Small drops together make a big ocean.
  • Small grains together make land.
  • Kind words and warm deeds spread happiness.
  • Small good acts make the world beautiful.
