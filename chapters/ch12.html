How Much Can We Spend?

Money

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Riya counts coins of ₹1, ₹2, ₹5 and ₹10 — Sahil counts notes of ₹10, ₹20, ₹50 and ₹100. At Rahim Kaka's toy shop we learn how much things cost!

  • Coins: ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10. Notes: ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100.
  • Four ₹5 coins make ₹20 — the same as two ₹10 notes.
  • We can make one amount in different ways: ₹13 = ₹10 + ₹3.
  • Saving money is a good habit!
