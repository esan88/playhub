Throwing and Catching

Throw far, catch safe

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Aim at the wall — whoosh! The ball flies straight. Eyes on the ball, hands ready — CATCH! Throwing and catching is super fun! 🤾

Throw with one hand, step forward, follow through. Catch with soft hands and eyes open.

  • Stand sideways and aim before throwing.
  • Step forward as you release the ball.
  • Watch the ball all the way in.
  • Catch with soft, cupped hands.
