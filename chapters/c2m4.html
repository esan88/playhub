Shadow Story (Togalu)

2D shapes & shadows

📖 What you will learn

After watching Togalu shadow puppets from Karnataka, make your own finger shadows! Trace bottle caps and boxes, and meet the flat 2-D family: circle, rectangle, triangle and square.

  • Light makes a shadow behind an object.
  • Tracing an object gives a flat 2D shape.
  • A circle is all curved with no corners.
  • Triangles and squares have straight sides and corners.
