Fifty-two seconds, one nation, hearts beating together! When the anthem plays, we stand straight and sing as one. 🇮🇳

Jana Gana Mana is our National Anthem, written by Rabindranath Tagore.

  • Stand at attention during the anthem.
  • Sing together in one voice.
  • The anthem honours our nation.
  • Remove caps and stand still.
