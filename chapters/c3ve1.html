Seekhne ki anandmayi kavita

📖 What you will learn

Chidiya chehchahati hai, phool muskuraate hain — sab kuchh kuchh sikhata hai! Aankhen kholo, Seekho kavita gungunao! 📖🐦

Seekho kavita kehti hai — har din kuchh naya seekho. Prakriti sabse achchhi teacher hai.

  • Chidiya se meetha bolna seekho.
  • Phoolon se muskuraana seekho.
  • Roz kuchh naya seekhne ki aadat dalo.
  • Seekhne mein kabhi sharmana nahin.
