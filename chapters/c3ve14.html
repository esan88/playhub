किसान की होशियारी

Hoshiyar kisan ki kahani

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Sookha pada, par kisan ne himmat na haari — nayi vidhi, mehnat, bachat! Khet lehlaha utha. Hoshiyari jeet gayi! 🚜🌾

Kisan annadata hai. Mehnat aur hoshiyari se mushkil aasaan hoti hai.

  • Kisan khet mein ann ugaata hai.
  • Mehnat ka phal meetha hota hai.
  • Hoshiyari se sankat talta hai.
  • Ann ka ek dana bhi na vyarth karo.
