Seasons & clothes

📖 What you will learn

Summer is hot, winter is cold, monsoon clouds are filled with rain and spring makes flowers bloom. Learn what each season brings and the clothes we wear — cotton frocks in summer, woolen caps in winter and umbrellas in the monsoon!

  • Summer is HOT — everyone wants a dip in the pool.
  • Spring: flowers bloom every day. Winter: people enjoy the sun’s rays.
  • Monsoon clouds are filled with RAIN — carry an umbrella!
  • Clothes: frock, shirt, cap, sari, dhoti-kurta, woolen cap, t-shirt.
