The moon is seen and Eid comes with joy. New clothes, sweet dishes and hugs are everywhere. Let us learn how Eid is loved.

This essay tells about festivals of India like Eid, Holi, Diwali and Christmas. It explains Roza, Sehri, Iftar, Fitra and Zakat. Eid gives a message of joy and sharing.

  • Eid comes after the month of Ramzan fasts.
  • Sehri is eaten early and Iftar opens the fast.
  • People wear new clothes and pray the Eid prayer.
  • Children get Eidi and enjoy the fair rides.
