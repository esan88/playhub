Lalu loved by all

📖 What you will learn

Lalu comes crying with tears on his face. Dadi, Amma, Apa and Bhabhi hug him close. Loved Lalu is the star of all eyes.

This poem by Barkat Ali Firaq is full of family love. Each member comforts Lalu with songs, bread and sweets. Lalu belongs to every one.

  • Lalu comes home crying with tears.
  • Brother, Dadi and Amma ask with love.
  • Apa gives bread and sweets to eat.
  • Lalu is dear to all and star of eyes.
