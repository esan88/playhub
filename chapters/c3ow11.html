📖 What you will learn

Mini watches Appooppan the potter — wet clay, pound, shape, dry, bake! A soft lump becomes a strong pot. Magic of hands! 🏺

Potters shape clay into pots, diyas and kulhads. Each step comes in order.

  • Clay is mixed with water till soft.
  • Pounding removes air bubbles.
  • The wheel shapes the wet clay.
  • Sun-dry, then bake to make it strong.
