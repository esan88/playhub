نیم کا پیڑ

Neem tree uses tale

📖 What you will learn

Rashid goes to cut the neem tree and rests in its shade. An old man tells him the leaves heal and clean. Rashid promises never to cut trees.

This story lists neem gifts like shade, cool air, clean grain and medicine. Dry leaves guard clothes, oil heals skin and miswak cleans teeth. Every tree has some virtue.

  • Rashid rests tired under the neem shade.
  • Neem leaves stop heat and give cool air.
  • Dry leaves guard clothes and grain.
  • Rashid promises to guard all trees.
