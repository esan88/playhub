“Dekho! Rocket udaan bhare!” — bachche samvaad mein Chandrayaan ki kahani kehte hain. Vigyaan bhi, masti bhi! 🚀🌙

Chandrayaan Bharat ka Chandrayaan hai. Samvaad mein baatcheet se seekho.

  • Chandrayaan Chaand par gaya.
  • Vigyaan desh ko aage badhata hai.
  • Samvaad mein sawaal-jawaab hote hain.
  • Sapne dekho, mehnat karo.
