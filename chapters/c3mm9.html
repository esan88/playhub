House of Hundreds – II

Comparing 3-digit numbers

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Every house on the street has a 3-digit number. Which house is number 358? Line them up and compare, room by room! 🏡

Compare hundreds first. If tied, compare tens. If still tied, compare ones.

  • Bigger hundreds digit = bigger number.
  • 326 vs 362: same hundreds, 6 tens wins.
  • Use >, < and = to compare numbers.
  • Order numbers smallest to biggest step by step.
