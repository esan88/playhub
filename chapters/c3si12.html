Football team game

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Football is played on a wide flat field with two teams. Kick the ball and guard your goal. The referee watches every rule.

This essay explains teams, captain, goal keeper and referee. Each team has eleven players and one goal. Brazil and Kolkata love this game.

  • Football is played only with the feet.
  • Each team has eleven players and a captain.
  • Only the goal keeper may use hands.
  • The referee decision must be obeyed.
