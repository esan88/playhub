Night moon and stars

📖 What you will learn

Night spreads its wings over our heads. The moon smiles and stars twinkle bright. Sleep now and wash away all tiredness.

This short poem shows night after sunny day. No sun, no heat, only cool rest. Children read, sleep and wake fresh.

  • Night comes and covers the sky.
  • Moon and stars shine and glow.
  • Day heat and sunshine go away.
  • Sleep on the bed and rest well.
