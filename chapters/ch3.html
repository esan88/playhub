Mango Treat

Counting 1 to 9

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Birds and animals gather for a mango treat — one joins, then another! Every time one more joins, the count grows by one. That is how we learn numbers 1 to 9.

  • Count objects by touching each one once: 1, 2, 3…
  • "One more" means the next number — one more than 6 is 7.
  • Numbers go in order: after 8 comes 9, before 4 comes 3.
  • We can find which group has more, less or equal.
