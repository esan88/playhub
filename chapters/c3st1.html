Colours

Naming colours around us

📖 What you will learn

Meena opens her colour box — wow! Every crayon has a name. Let’s paint the world and learn each colour’s name! 🖍️

Colours have names: red, yellow, blue, green, orange, purple, pink, brown, black, white.

  • Say the colour before the thing — “red apple”.
  • The sun is yellow, the sky is blue, leaves are green.
  • Mix red and yellow to imagine orange!
  • Ask “What colour is it?” to learn new colours.
