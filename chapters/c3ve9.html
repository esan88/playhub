Phoolon ka tyohar Phooldei

📖 What you will learn

Basant aaya! Bachche tokri mein phool lekar dwar-dwar jaate, geet gaate — Phooldei mubarak! 🌸🧺

Phooldei Uttarakhand ka phoolon wala tyohar hai. Prakriti ka dhanyavaad kehte hain.

  • Phooldei Basant ritu mein manaya jaata hai.
  • Bachche phoolon se dwar sajaate hain.
  • Lokgeet gaaye jaate hain.
  • Prakriti ka shukriya ada karo.
