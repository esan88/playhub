So Many Toys

More, less or equal

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

So many toys in the shop! Dolls, cars, elephants, teddy bears — which group has more? In this chapter we compare using three magic phrases: more than, less than, equal to.

  • Count both groups first, then compare.
  • More than: 7 is more than 5. Less than: 4 is less than 9.
  • Equal to: 5 + 3 and 4 + 4 are equal — both are 8.
  • The group with the smallest count is the LEAST in number.
