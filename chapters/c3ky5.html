Local and Traditional Games

Kho-kho, kabaddi, gilli-danda

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Grandma’s games beat phone games! Kho-kho chase, kabaddi “kabaddi-kabaddi”, gilli flying high — our very own treasures! 🪁

Traditional games need no costly gear — just friends and open space.

  • Kho-kho: chase and touch to win.
  • Kabaddi: raid chanting “kabaddi”.
  • Gilli-danda: flick the small stick far.
  • Pitthu, marbles and kites are classics too.
