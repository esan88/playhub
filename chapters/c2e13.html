We are all Indians

Unity in diversity + charkha play

📖 What you will learn

From Gujarat to Kerala, Punjab to Bengal — children introduce their language, festival, dance, food and place! All are different, yet all are Indians. The chapter ends with a short play on the charkha.

  • India has many languages like Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali.
  • Garba, Bhangra and puppet shows are Indian arts.
  • Pongal and Phool Dei are festivals that celebrate nature.
  • Unity in diversity means different but one India.
