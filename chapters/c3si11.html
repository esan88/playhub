Ramu honesty folk tale

☆☆☆Best score 0

📖 What you will learn

Ramu is poor but honest and loves his cow. Famine leaves no fodder to eat. His honesty wins a kind gift.

This folk tale lives near the Ganga river. Ramu takes fodder at night but his heart speaks. The field owner sees all and forgives with love.

  • Ramu is poor but honest and hardworking.
  • Famine leaves no fodder for his cow.
  • Honesty is the biggest virtue he keeps.
  • The owner gifts fodder in the morning.
